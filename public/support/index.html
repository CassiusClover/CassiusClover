Contact the publisher

For player support, bug reports, accessibility issues, privacy requests, or questions about the game, email symbioticsolutionsai@gmail.com.

Publisher: Symbiotic Solutions Limited
Country: United States
Audience: Ages 13 and older

For bug reports, include your device model, operating-system version, app version, what happened, and how to reproduce it. Screenshots are helpful if you remove personal information. Do not send passwords or sensitive device identifiers.

Erase progress and delete data

In the app, open Settings → Erase all progress, then tap again to confirm. This resets game progress, including tips, unlocks, upgrades, and your journal. This action cannot be undone.

Downloaded AI models are managed separately. Use the Delete button next to each downloaded model in the Dungeon Master settings. To remove remaining local app data, use your device’s clear-app-data option where available or delete the app. Offloading may retain data, and device backups must be managed separately.

There is no game account to delete and no developer-operated cloud-save recovery. Resetting the game does not remove information independently retained by ad providers. See privacy choices and requests for more information.

Ads and rewards

Rewarded ads are optional and require a connection. If an ad does not load, you can continue playing and try again later. Rewards depend on the ad service confirming completion. Advertising availability can vary by region and device.

Use any consent choices presented by the app and your device’s privacy controls to manage advertising preferences. Contact us for help with privacy requests; see our advertising disclosure.

AI Dungeon Master downloads

The optional AI Dungeon Master needs a supported native app build and enough device storage. Model downloads are approximately 469 MB or 1.1 GB and may incur mobile data charges. If a download fails, check your connection and available storage before retrying.

The AI model runs locally once downloaded. You can disable it in settings and keep using the scripted Dungeon Master. The website encounter preview does not use the AI model.

Policies and release information

Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. The game is coming to iOS and Android; store availability and ratings may differ by region. Follow development through the link on the home page.
