Who we are

Symbiotic Solutions LLC, United States, publishes Side Quest Simulator. This policy covers the mobile game and its public website. For privacy questions or requests, contact symbioticsolutionsai@gmail.com.

Information we process

Game progress and preferences. The game stores your current run, unlocks, tips, upgrades, journal, and settings locally on your device. There is no game account or developer-operated cloud save service in the current version. Device or operating-system backups may retain app data under your own backup settings.

Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments or diagnostic information you choose to include. Please do not send passwords, advertising identifiers, or other sensitive information.

Website requests. Our hosting provider, Netlify, processes technical information such as IP addresses, requested pages, timestamps, browser information, and security logs to deliver and protect the website. The site does not include a first-party analytics tracker, advertising SDK, account registration, or signup form. Its dice preview runs in your browser without sending your choices to us.

Rewarded advertising in the app

The native mobile game integrates Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads. Choosing to watch an ad can grant an in-game reward. The ad SDK may initialize when the app starts, so declining a rewarded ad does not necessarily prevent all SDK processing.

Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses, advertising or device identifiers where permitted, device and app information, approximate location inferred from an IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. They use this information to deliver, measure, secure, and, where permitted, personalize advertising. Processing depends on platform settings, region, consent, and the released app configuration.

The app includes Google’s consent flow where supported and required. Use the choices presented there and your device’s privacy and advertising controls. You may decline optional rewarded ads and continue playing. Changing advertising settings does not necessarily stop processing needed for contextual ads, fraud prevention, or security.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps and sites. This website itself does not display AdMob ads.

Optional on-device AI

You can optionally download a language model for the AI Dungeon Master from Hugging Face. Download requests disclose technical connection information, including your IP address, to Hugging Face and its delivery providers. See the Hugging Face Privacy Policy.

Once downloaded, the model generates narration on your device. The game does not send gameplay prompts or generated narration to a cloud AI service. Downloaded model files remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app’s data.

Website services and external links

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to request those fonts, disclosing technical information including your IP address and request headers. See Google Fonts privacy information.

Hosting is provided by Netlify; see Netlify’s Privacy Statement. Links to GitHub, app stores, and other external services take you to services governed by their own policies. This site does not set first-party analytics or advertising cookies. Service providers may process technical data needed to deliver their services.

Purposes and legal grounds

We process information to provide the game and website, save progress on your device, respond to support and privacy requests, deliver optional features, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations. Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on performance of our service agreement for requested functionality, legitimate interests for support and security, legal obligations where applicable, and consent for processing that requires it, including certain advertising activities. You may withdraw consent without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Sharing and international processing

We do not operate a business selling your saved game progress or support messages. Service providers process information to deliver hosting, email, ads, fonts, and model downloads. Advertising partners may process data in ways treated as a “sale,” “sharing,” or targeted advertising under some privacy laws, depending on the actual configuration and your choices. Use available consent and platform controls and contact us for assistance with an applicable opt-out request.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer subject to applicable privacy requirements. Providers may process information in the United States and other countries with different privacy laws. Where applicable, legally required safeguards apply to international transfers.

Retention and security

Local saves persist until overwritten or deleted. Downloaded models persist until deleted. We retain support correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain relevant support records, and meet legal obligations; we then delete or anonymize it. Provider-held logs and advertising data follow the providers’ retention policies.

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information we handle. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, and local saves should not be treated as secure storage for sensitive information.

Your choices and privacy rights

In the game, open Settings → Erase all progress and confirm to reset progress. Downloaded AI models have a separate Delete control in the Dungeon Master settings. To remove app-local data more broadly, use your operating system’s clear-app-data controls where available or delete the app. Offloading an app may retain its data. Manage device backups separately; resetting the game does not erase data already held by advertising or other service providers.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain advertising uses. Contact symbioticsolutionsai@gmail.com with your request and relevant context. We may reasonably verify your identity, respond within legally required periods, and explain any applicable exceptions. You may use an authorized agent where permitted. We do not discriminate for exercising privacy rights. If an appeal is available under your local law, email us with the subject “Privacy appeal.” You may also complain to your local privacy regulator.

We cannot retrieve local-only game saves from your device. For data independently held by Google or another provider, its privacy tools may be the appropriate way to exercise your rights.

Players under 18

Side Quest Simulator is intended for ages 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Players below the age of legal adulthood should use the service with a parent or guardian’s permission. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, contact symbioticsolutionsai@gmail.com so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

The intended audience is separate from any age rating assigned by an app store. Advertising remains subject to applicable consent, age, and platform restrictions.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. We post the updated policy with a revised date and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required. Questions and requests: symbioticsolutionsai@gmail.com. Publisher: Symbiotic Solutions LLC, United States.
