QUEST example-count-the-sheep
title: Count the Baron's Sheep
task: count a baron's sheep before he wakes from his nap
art: marrow

SCENE
The sheep will not stand still. One of them is a dog in a fleece and thinks you have not noticed.
> Careful WIS: Count legs and divide by four
  win: Four hundred legs, one hundred sheep, and one dog you agree not to mention.
  lose: You count the dog. The dog counts you. It gets awkward.
> Bold CHA: Ask the sheep to form a line
  win: They do. Nobody is more surprised than the sheep.
  lose: They form a circle, with you in the middle.

FINALE
The baron wakes up early and demands the number. You have a number. It is not a number you are proud of.
> Bold CHA: Say it with total confidence
  win: "One hundred and one." He nods. He never knew either.
  lose: "About some." He was hoping for more than that.
> Careful INT: Show your working
  win: Four pages of tally marks. He is too impressed to check them.
  lose: He checks them.
> Reckless STR: Bring him the sheep instead
  win: All of them, at once, in his bedroom. The count is undeniable.
  lose: All of them, at once, on his foot.
