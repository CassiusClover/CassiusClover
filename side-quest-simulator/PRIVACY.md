# Side Quest Simulator Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Side Quest Simulator ("the app") is a single-player game for iOS and Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

## What the app stores on your device

Your heroes, progress, settings and journal are saved only on your device. The app has no user accounts, and the developer does not receive or keep a copy of your saves. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Advertising

The app offers optional rewarded video ads, shown only when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward. These ads are provided by Google AdMob.

When an ad is requested, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device type, operating system and app interaction data, to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where permitted, personalize ads. Google's use of this information is governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and its explanation of how it uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, the app asks for your choice through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice later from the app's settings, or reset your device's advertising identifier in your device's settings.

## Optional on-device AI narrator

If you turn on the optional AI Dungeon Master, the app downloads a language model file once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Hugging Face receives the standard information included in any download request, such as your IP address. After the download, the narrator runs entirely on your device. Your game text is not sent to the developer or to any AI service.

## What the app does not do

The app does not use analytics, crash-reporting services or social logins, and it does not sell your personal information. The developer does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to content suitable for teen audiences or lower.
